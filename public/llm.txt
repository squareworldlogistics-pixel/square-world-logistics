## Operations & Compliance Information

### Standard Incoterms Supported
*   **EXW (Ex Works)**: Buyer handles all transit costs and risks from the seller's premises.
*   **FOB (Free On Board)**: Seller delivers goods on board; risk transfers at ship's rail.
*   **CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight)**: Seller pays port delivery and basic insurance; risk transfers at origin.
*   **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)**: Seller handles all transport, duties, taxes, and risks to the destination.
*   **DAP (Delivered At Place)**: Seller delivers to destination; buyer manages customs clearance and import duties.

### Prohibited Items (Cannot be Shipped)
*   Narcotic Drugs & Psychotropic Substances
*   Firearms, Ammunition & Weapons
*   Explosives & Flammable Materials
*   Radioactive & Corrosive Substances
*   Currency, Coins & Bullion
*   Precious Stones & High-Value Jewellery
*   Live Animals & Rare Plant Species
*   Counterfeit or Obscene Materials
*   Hazardous Waste & Toxic Chemicals
